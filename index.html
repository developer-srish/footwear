About Us

Pranab Paul & Dipa Paul Enterprise was established with a simple vision — to bring quality footwear to the people of Malda at honest prices. What started as a small shop has grown into a trusted name, serving thousands of families across English Bazar and surrounding areas.

Our Promise

Every pair of shoes we sell is handpicked for quality. We stock from reliable manufacturers and ensure that both durability and style are never compromised. Comfort is our priority — because you deserve the best for every step.

Wholesale & Retail

We cater to both individual buyers and bulk wholesale orders. Whether you're shopping for your family or stocking your store, we offer competitive pricing with the same commitment to quality at every scale.
